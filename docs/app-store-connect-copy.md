## Description
Mrktfy helps you discover and organize real estate listings that match your needs.

Search nearby properties, compare options, save favorites, and build shortlist decks to focus on the homes that matter most to you. Use location-aware browsing, buyer preferences, and property alerts to stay on top of new opportunities as they appear.

Features:
- Browse and compare property listings
- Save and organize shortlist candidates
- View property details, timelines, and matching insights
- Use location-based search to find nearby listings
- Receive alerts for properties that fit your criteria
- Sign in with email/password, Google, or Apple

Mrktfy is designed to make property discovery faster, clearer, and more personalized.
